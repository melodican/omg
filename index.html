About us

Meet Glen & Gemma

OMG! Live is the two of us, a pair of experienced vocalists and hosts based in Blackpool. We know a great night out needs more than a playlist. It needs people who can get a crowd singing, laughing and up on their feet.

We've spent plenty of nights on stage and behind the mic, so we know how to read a room: when to pack the dance floor, when to hand the mic to the crowd and when to let a big moment breathe. Book OMG! Live and you get two seasoned performers who care about making your night a great one.
